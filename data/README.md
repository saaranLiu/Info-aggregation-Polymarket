# Data samples and provenance

This directory contains small examples drawn from the study's **saved market-level observations and estimates**. No synthetic trades or reconstructed trade-level midpoints are included.

## Included files

| File in `samples/` | Unit | Source in `results/` | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `polymarket_windows.csv` | One market × observation window | `mkt_A_windows_YesNo.csv` | Inspect outcomes, prices, small/large cash imbalances and event clusters |
| `sports_moneyline_windows.csv` | One market × observation window | `mkt_A_windows_SportsML.csv` | Equivalent sports-moneyline example |
| `heldout_predictions.csv` | Market × analysis cell × window | `joint_flow_predictions.csv` | Check saved out-of-fold predictions and paired Brier differences |
| `settlement_estimates.csv` | One metric × analysis cell × sample | `paper_estimates.csv` | Verify the two accounting identities on retained estimates |
| `kalshi_margins.csv` | One size bin or contrast | `k_B_margin.csv` | Absolute/relative cash bins, trade/dollar weights and event-cluster SEs |
| `kalshi_predictions.csv` | One observed-flow cohort | `k_C_prediction.csv` | Separate small-flow and both-flow forward prediction results |
| `manifest.json` | Provenance record | Generated by `analysis/build_samples.py` | Source hashes, selection rule, counts and output hashes |

The Polymarket sample retains **1,483 Yes/No markets** and **200 sports-moneyline markets**, together with their available observation windows. The selection chooses up to 100 eligible markets per topic/outcome stratum by ascending SHA-256 of the market identifier. Eligibility is evaluated in the history ending 24 hours before resolution: at least 20 window trades, observed small and large imbalances, and an interior window-end price.

This is an outcome-stratified demonstration sample. Its Brier scores and outcome frequencies are **not representative population estimates**, and it does not reproduce all transaction-level results. Use the complete saved observations in `results/` for the market-level refit, and the original trade/quote archives for trade-level estimation.

## Definitions

- `condition_id`: public market identifier, not a wallet identifier.
- `cat`, `Y`: event topic and binary settlement payoff.
- `ev_cluster`: topic × on-chain resolution date for Polymarket inference.
- `win_name`: lifetime before 24 hours, 48-to-24 hours, or seven-days-to-24-hours window.
- `n_win`, `usd_win`: window trade count and dollar notional.
- `imb_small`, `imb_large`, `imb_all`: direction-signed dollar imbalance, bounded by −1 and +1. Small means below $100; large means at least $1,000.
- `p_post`, `p_pre`: midpoint proxies at the window's end/start; unavailable values remain blank.
- `pred_*`, `fold`: saved outcome probabilities and event-grouped cross-validation folds. Folds are defined within their original group/cell/window, not globally across all rows.
- Estimate files ending in `_bp` use probability basis points. In raw prediction summaries `dbrier`, `lo`, `hi` remain unscaled Brier units. Manuscript tables multiply gains by 10,000.

Kalshi's clock is exchange close; Polymarket's main clock is on-chain resolution. Kalshi inference clusters by `event_ticker`. Its two prediction rows use separate observed-flow cohorts, and cannot be treated as a nested common-sample model comparison.

## Recreate and check

```sh
python3 analysis/build_samples.py
bash analysis/run.sh check
```

The check verifies exact correspondence to the saved sources, accounting identities, event-fold integrity and the manuscript's saved numerical summaries. Demo output goes to ignored `work/sample_demo/`.

## Original sources and access

The Polymarket transaction archive is described by Qin and Yang, *Polymarket-v1 Database*, [arXiv:2606.04217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04217). The quoted-book checks use the [pmxt/Hugging Face order-book archive](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Joseph3222/polymarket-orderbook) and the Dome dataset identified in `analysis/31_d1_genesis_quotes.py`. Kalshi results use `TrevorJS/kalshi-trades`, pinned to revision `5c622baffd` in `analysis/32_d4_kalshi.py`.

Follow each original source's license and attribution requirements. This repository does not assign a blanket new license to third-party data. Full transaction archives, quote snapshots and large intermediate event tables are not bundled. The three local event Parquet files available during packaging failed format-integrity checks and were excluded; none was converted into a purported trade sample.

`results/SOURCES.json` records hashes and conventions for the retained result files. `DATA_MANIFEST.json` in this directory records hashes and sizes of the data files shipped in the GitHub snapshot.
